RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT (VERSION 1.0)

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Agreement") is made and entered into on this 1st day of April, 2026, by and between:

LANDLORD: Mr. Rajesh Kumar, residing at Flat 402, Green Meadows, Bengaluru, Karnataka, India ("Landlord")
AND
TENANT: Ms. Priya Sharma, currently residing at HSR Layout, Bengaluru, Karnataka, India ("Tenant").

The Landlord and Tenant are collectively referred to as the "Parties."

--- [Page 1] ---
1. PREMISES AND TERM
1.1 The Landlord hereby leases to the Tenant the residential property located at Apartment 204, Palm Heights, Outer Ring Road, Bellandur, Bengaluru - 560103 ("Premises").
1.2 The lease term shall commence on May 1, 2026 ("Commencement Date") and continue for a period of eleven (11) consecutive months, expiring on March 31, 2027 ("Expiration Date"), unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Agreement.

2. RENT AND PAYMENT TERMS
2.1 The monthly rent for the Premises shall be ₹25,000 (Twenty-Five Thousand Indian Rupees), payable in advance on or before the 5th day of each calendar month via direct bank transfer to the Landlord's designated bank account.
2.2 If rent is not received by the 10th of the month, a late fee of ₹500 shall be assessed. Continued failure to pay rent for more than 15 consecutive days constitutes a material breach.

--- [Page 2] ---
3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
3.1 The Tenant shall deposit with the Landlord a refundable security deposit of ₹1,00,000 (One Lakh Indian Rupees) upon signing this Agreement.
3.2 The security deposit shall be held without interest and refunded to the Tenant within twenty-one (21) business days following the complete vacation of the Premises, subject to deductions for unpaid utilities or physical damages beyond reasonable wear and tear.

4. UTILITIES AND MAINTENANCE RESPONSIBILITIES
4.1 The Tenant shall be solely responsible for payment of all electricity, water, internet, and municipal utility charges incurred during the tenancy.
4.2 Minor day-to-day repairs costing less than ₹1,000 per occurrence (such as light bulb replacement, minor plumbing washers) shall be borne by the Tenant.
4.3 Major structural repairs, electrical wiring failures not caused by tenant negligence, and exterior roofing maintenance shall be the sole responsibility of the Landlord.

--- [Page 3] ---
5. USE OF PREMISES AND RESTRICTIONS
5.1 The Premises shall be used exclusively for private residential purposes by the Tenant and immediate family members. Subletting, assignment, or commercial use without prior written consent from the Landlord is strictly prohibited.
5.2 The Tenant shall maintain peaceful occupancy and not cause nuisance or disturbance to neighboring residents.

6. TERMINATION AND NOTICE PERIOD
6.1 Either Party may terminate this Agreement prior to the Expiration Date by providing at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the other Party.
6.2 If the Tenant vacates without providing the required 30 days' notice, the Landlord may forfeit one month's rent from the security deposit.

7. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION
7.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India.
7.2 In the event of any dispute arising out of this Agreement, the Parties agree to resolve the matter through mutual discussion. If unresolved, the competent courts in Bengaluru, Karnataka shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have executed this Agreement as of the date first above written.
