RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT (VERSION 2.0 - REVISED)

This Revised Residential Lease Agreement ("Agreement") is made and entered into on this 15th day of April, 2026, by and between:

LANDLORD: Mr. Rajesh Kumar, residing at Flat 402, Green Meadows, Bengaluru, Karnataka, India ("Landlord")
AND
TENANT: Ms. Priya Sharma, currently residing at HSR Layout, Bengaluru, Karnataka, India ("Tenant").

The Landlord and Tenant are collectively referred to as the "Parties."

--- [Page 1] ---
1. PREMISES AND TERM
1.1 The Landlord hereby leases to the Tenant the residential property located at Apartment 204, Palm Heights, Outer Ring Road, Bellandur, Bengaluru - 560103 ("Premises").
1.2 The lease term shall commence on May 1, 2026 ("Commencement Date") and continue for a period of twelve (12) consecutive months, expiring on April 30, 2027 ("Expiration Date"), with automatic month-to-month renewal unless written notice is served.

2. RENT AND PAYMENT TERMS
2.1 The monthly rent for the Premises shall be ₹28,500 (Twenty-Eight Thousand Five Hundred Indian Rupees), payable in advance on or before the 3rd day of each calendar month via direct bank transfer to the Landlord's designated bank account.
2.2 If rent is not received by the 5th of the month, a late fee of ₹1,500 shall be assessed immediately.

--- [Page 2] ---
3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
3.1 The Tenant shall deposit with the Landlord a refundable security deposit of ₹1,20,000 (One Lakh Twenty Thousand Indian Rupees) upon signing this Agreement.
3.2 The security deposit shall be held without interest and refunded within forty-five (45) business days following complete vacation and inspection of the Premises.
3.3 A mandatory professional deep-cleaning deduction of ₹5,000 shall be deducted from the security deposit upon move-out regardless of condition.

4. UTILITIES AND MAINTENANCE RESPONSIBILITIES
4.1 The Tenant shall be solely responsible for payment of all electricity, water, internet, and building maintenance association surcharges.
4.2 Minor repairs costing less than ₹2,500 per occurrence shall be borne exclusively by the Tenant.
4.3 Structural repairs shall be performed by Landlord subject to written notice delivered within 48 hours of occurrence.

--- [Page 3] ---
5. USE OF PREMISES AND RESTRICTIONS
5.1 The Premises shall be used exclusively for private residential purposes. No pets, subletting, or overnight guests exceeding 7 consecutive days are permitted without prior written consent.

6. TERMINATION AND NOTICE PERIOD
6.1 Either Party may terminate this Agreement prior to the Expiration Date by providing at least sixty (60) days' prior written notice to the other Party.
6.2 In the event of termination before 6 months of occupancy, the Tenant agrees to forfeit 50% of the security deposit as liquidated damages.

7. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION
7.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India, and exclusive jurisdiction resides in the courts of Bengaluru, Karnataka.
